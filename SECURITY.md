# Security notes and responsible use

PFIV Guard is an academic research prototype. It is not a production payment-security service.

- Do not submit real card numbers, CVV, OTPs, passwords, PINs, bank credentials, or live payment forms.
- The app performs passive public-page retrieval and does not submit forms or initiate payments.
- Do not use the app to scan private networks or internal services.
- The project has not undergone an independent penetration test.
- If you identify a security issue, report it privately to the project maintainers and do not publish target details or exploit instructions.
